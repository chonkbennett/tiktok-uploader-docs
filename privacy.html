Privacy Policy - Fairy Odd Videos Uploader

Last updated July 31, 2026

Who operates this

This is a private, single-operator automation tool. It connects to exactly one TikTok account, owned and controlled by the operator, and is not made available to any other user.

What data is accessed

  • TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to authenticate scheduled uploads to the operator's own account
  • Basic account info (open id, avatar, display name) via user.info.basic, used to confirm the correct account is connected
  • Video files from the operator's local library, which is the content being uploaded

What is not collected

  • No data from any third party or other TikTok user
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers
  • No data is sold or shared with any other service

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored locally by the operator and used only to make authenticated calls to TikTok's Content Posting API. They are retained until the operator revokes the app's access from their TikTok account settings.
